key expiry

Every key has an expires_at timestamp set at upload time. Guest and free keys use their plan maximum automatically. Paid plans let you choose any duration up to the plan maximum.

locked files

On guest and free plans, anyone who knows a key can access its content. This is intentional for quick ephemeral sharing.

Paid files are permanently locked to your account the moment you create them. Only you can edit, rename, delete, or manage them.

storage quota

Your usage is shown on your account page. Uploads that would push you over quota are rejected immediately with a clear error. Deleting a file frees the storage instantly.

password protection

Paid accounts can add a password to any file they own. Stored as a PBKDF2 hash — never in plain text. Browsers remember unlocked files for the session. The CLI prompts automatically.

folders

Folders let you group files under your drive path: /@username/folder-name/. Every plan gets unlimited folders. Pro accounts can activate path access on specific folders to let others browse them — individual password-protected files still require their password when opened.

billing

Paid plans bill monthly via Lemon Squeezy. Upgrade from your account page. Cancelling returns you to the free plan at the end of the billing period — files are not deleted, but paid-only limits no longer apply to new uploads. Manage or cancel anytime in the billing portal.
