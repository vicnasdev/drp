expiry

Anonymous & free clipboard drops idle out — they don't expire on a fixed timer, they expire when nobody reads them. Anonymous drops idle after 24 h and are hard-deleted at 7 days. Free account drops idle after 48 h with a 30-day hard cap.

File drops (anonymous and free) expire 90 days after upload regardless of access.

Paid drops let you set an explicit date — up to 1 year on Starter, up to 3 years on Pro. They can be renewed at any time, pushing the expiry forward by the same duration. No renewal limit.

locked drops

On anonymous and free plans, anyone who knows your key can overwrite its content after the 24-hour post-creation window. This is intentional for quick ephemeral sharing.

Paid drops are permanently locked to your account the moment you create them. Only you can edit, rename, delete, or renew them.

storage quota

Quota counts file drops only — text clipboards are free. Your usage is shown on your account page. Uploads that would push you over quota are rejected immediately with a clear error. Deleting a file drop frees the storage instantly.

password protection

Paid accounts can add a password to any drop they own. Stored as a PBKDF2 hash — never in plain text. Browsers remember unlocked drops for the session. The CLI prompts automatically.

billing

Paid plans bill monthly via Lemon Squeezy. Upgrade from your account page. Cancelling returns you to the free plan at the end of the billing period — drops are not deleted, but paid-only limits no longer apply to new uploads. Manage or cancel anytime in the billing portal.
